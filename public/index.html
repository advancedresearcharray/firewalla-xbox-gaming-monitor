How path enforcement works

After probing, Firewalla drops Xbox traffic to slower alternate IPs (e.g. slow IPv6 when IPv4 is faster) and to datacenter probe endpoints more than 35 ms worse than your best region. Warzone and Destiny 2 game servers are probed automatically. Rules apply to Xbox source IP/MAC only — laptops and phones keep default router paths.
